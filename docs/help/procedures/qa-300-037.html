1. Scope

This procedure applies to Enflite personnel responsible for the disposition, use, and management of the MRR, CMR, and TRR forms within Syteline.

2. Purpose

To establish a standardized procedure and process flow for processing MRR’s, CMR’s, and TRR’s in Syteline.

3. Reference

QA 300-008Receiving Inspection
QA 300-010Material Review Board
QA 300-029Material Review
QA 300-066Streamlined Rework Nonconformance Process
QA 300-003Supplier Deviation Request
ENG 310-005Engineering Orders – Policy, Process and Work Instruction

4. Definitions

MRR
Material Review Report; a Syteline quality control form used to manage nonconforming, suspected, or damaged materials, including documentation, review, and disposition (e.g., scrap, rework, return to vendor).
CMR
ChangedChange Management Request; the “eCMRs” Syteline form used to manage deviations, clarifications, and process gaps identified by vendors, customers, and Enflite internal personnel. It replaces the Create Change Request, Change Request Management and QC CMRs forms.Rev wasChange Management Request; A specialized Syteline quality control form used to manage deviations, clarifications, and process gaps identified by vendors, customers, and Enflite internal personnel.WhyCMRs are now one form, eCMRs.
TRR
Topic Review Request; A specialized Syteline quality control form used to track and manage errors by Enflite production personnel.
Streamline
Does not require detailed rework instructions or Engineering Disposition to return to type design. (e.g. Loose screws, missing washer, etc.)
Syteline
The Enflite ERP system used to manage end-to-end business operations.
ECO
Engineering Change Order: Used to explain changes to manufacturing documents.
NEI
Notice of Engineering Intent: Used to make minor changes to drawings to provide clarity or to address a discrepancy.
TS
Temporary Solution: Used to make minor temporary changes to a specific article or articles and provide valid acceptance.

5. Procedure

5.1. General Requirements

5.1.1.
All nonconformances, discrepancies, issues, deviations, clarifications, and process gaps will be documented in the applicable form outlined by the requirements of this procedure. Anything not defined in the process will be brought to the attention of the Quality Manager (or Designee).

5.2. Problem identification and verification

5.2.1.
For In-process discrepancy, a PIR will be generated by the Production Tech using PIR template, describing the issue along with pertinent details of part in question and job order.
5.2.2.
Quality will review and verify reported issue on the production floor prior to initiation of discrepancy in Syteline.
5.2.3.
Quality will determine if this PIR will be an MRR, CMR or TRR
5.2.3.1.
For issues that will include material transactions within Syteline, go to section 5.3 MMR Processing.
5.2.3.2.
For issues that does not require detailed rework instructions or Engineering Disposition to return to type design, go to section 5.4 TRR (Streamline) Processing.
5.2.3.3.
For issues that does not include material transactions, or to capture issues from the supplier (such as a SDR) go to section 5.5 CMR Processing.

5.3. MRR Processing

5.3.1.
Trace the affected part back to its original “Supplier Inspect/ Disposition” receiver. If the issue was identified during receiving inspection, proceed to Step 5.3.4. Otherwise, go to Step 5.3.2
5.3.2.
If the MRR is being created outside of the receiving inspection, open the “Supplier Inspect/Disposition” form and filter by the part’s lot number. Use the “IP Job Inspect/Disposition” form if the part was manufactured at Enflite. If the lot number is unavailable, locate it via the “Material Transactions” form and filtering by the affected part number and job number.
5.3.3.
If the part is associated with an IP Job, contact planning to open the job operation: otherwise, proceed to 5.3.4.
5.3.3.1.
Planning will open the applicable job operation used by the inspector and when complete will notify QC Inspector the job operation is open.
5.3.4.
Select “Disposition QC Receiver”.
5.3.5.
Enter the affected quantity in the QC MRR/Hold section of the Disposition Receiving form and input the appropriate reason code.
5.3.6.
Click “Process”, then click “OK” on the pop-up.
5.3.7.
Close the Qc Tags Report tab after making note of the MRR number.
5.3.8.
On the Syteline tab, click “OK” to confirm MRR creation.
5.3.9.
Click “OK” to confirm the quantity move.
5.3.10.
Verify the “to location” is correct, then click “Process.” If prompted that the location does not exist, click “OK” to add it.
5.3.11.
When prompted “[Quantity Move] was successful,” click “OK.” If the prompt does not appear, notify Quality Leadership.
5.3.12.
Open the MRR form using the part number and MRR number recorded in Step 5.3.7.
5.3.13.
Enter a clear problem description in the designated block. If created from an IP Job, include at minimum: job number, top-level part number, affected part number, lot number, and issue. Always state what the part should be versus what it is (e.g., “Part should be 3.00 ± 0.010 inches; part is 2.75 inches”). Include Sheet number and zone if applicable.
5.3.14.
Select the appropriate cause code from the drop-down menu.
5.3.15.
If the cause of defect is known, document it in the designated field.
5.3.16.
In the “Assigned To” field, select the appropriate party. If the issue is related to tooling or measurement capability, assign to the Quality Manager or designee; otherwise, assign to Engineering.
5.3.17.
If priority is required for processing, change Priority Field to one of the following (based on need by date)
5.3.17.1.
Priority 1, High. Select if ships or needs that day.
5.3.17.2.
Priority 2, Medium. Select if ships or needed within 24 hours.
5.3.17.3.
Priority 3 Low (default), for all others. Expected processing time within the week.
5.3.18.
Save the form.
5.3.19.
On Build Record, enter the MRR number for traceability within the Notes section at the end.
5.3.20.
Engineering/Quality reviews the issue and documents remarks in the Correction/Containment block, including initials and date.
5.3.21.
Engineering finalizes disposition in the Correction/Containment block, saves supporting documentation within IDM and reassigns the MRR to Planning/Purchasing. If an NEI or TS is used, it must first be approved by the Quality.
5.3.22.
Planning and Purchasing review the MRR. Planning enters disposition in the Correction/Containment block and shall not authorize rework or use-as-is if it conflicts with Engineering’s disposition.
5.3.23.
Planning/Purchasing reassigns the MRR to the originating Quality Inspector.
5.3.24.
The Quality Inspector reviews all information for accuracy.
5.3.25.
The Quality Inspector dispositions the MRR per instructions from Planning, Engineering, or Quality Manager.
5.3.26.
To disposition, click “Disposition MRR” and enter applicable information. If the part is to be returned to stock, complete the “Accepted” section; otherwise, complete the “Rejected” section.
5.3.27.
When accepting, always disposition as “MTS – Move to Stock,” then click “Process.”
5.3.28.
Print the QC Tags Report that appears, then close the tab.
5.3.29.
Click “OK” on the “Disposition Completed” pop-up. If it does not appear, contact Quality Leadership.
5.3.30.
Click “OK” two additional times.
5.3.31.
Select the appropriate destination location. For RTV, enter “QC RTV.” Otherwise, select QC Hold or inventory location.
5.3.32.
Click “Process”.
5.3.33.
Click “OK,” then enter the close date on the MRR and save the form. This completes the MRR process in Syteline.

5.4. TRR (Streamline) Processing

5.4.1.
For Open the “Create Quality Control Topics” form.
5.4.2.
Select “Production” from the initial topic drop-down.
5.4.3.
Enter the employee who made the error in “Reported By,” along with department, top-level item number, and work center.
5.4.4.
In the notes, at minimum state: who made the error, affected assembly, job number, and specific details of the error. Then click “Process.”
5.4.5.
Record the topic number from the new tab, close it, and navigate to that topic in “Quality Control Topics.”
5.4.6.
Select “X-Ref TRR” and record the generated TRR number.
5.4.7.
Open the TRR form and navigate to the recorded number.
5.4.8.
Set status to “Closed,” reason code to “STRMLINE,” re-enter the original note details in the description block, check “Close TRR,” and save. This completes the TRR.
