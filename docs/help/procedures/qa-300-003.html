1. Scope

This work instruction applies to Enflite. The Supplier Deviation Request work instruction does not cover the requirements of the Tech Help Work Instruction (ENG-310-301).

Department: Quality, Purchasing and Engineering

Job Position: Quality Control, Buyer and Designated Engineer

2. Purpose

This work instruction defines the process for suppliers to document any departure from drawing, specification, or other purchase order requirement and request disposition from Enflite. This policy supports the requirements of ISO 9001 and AS9100, as applicable.

3. Reference

5. WORK INSTRUCTION

5.1.
If a supplier has departed from drawing, specification, or other purchase order requirement with physical product (oversized hole, undersized length) that cannot be reworked to blueprint requirements, they shall fill out the supplier section of the Supplier Deviation Request, QA-400-005.
5.2.
If a supplier has a question on a drawing because of missing or incomplete information (dimensions, radius, material, etc.) or the part cannot be manufactured from the information given on the drawing, QA-400-005 may also be used for this purpose.
5.3.
The Supplier shall fill out the Supplier Section of the form, which includes Supplier Name, PO#, Part Number, Part Revision, Quantity, SDR Request Date, PO Promise Date, Requestor, Phone, and Email. A brief description of the deviation (or clarification) shall also be included, along with the requested action.
5.4.
Once the supplier has filled out the form, they shall submit it via email to Enflite SDR@enflite.com
5.5.
ChangedThe SDR received from the supplier will be entered into Syteline via the eCMRs form. For further details, please refer to QA-300-037 MRR, CMR, and TRR Processing.Rev wasThe SDR received from the supplier will be entered into Syteline via CMR form. For further details, please refer to QA-300-037 MRR, CMR, and TRR Processing.WhyCMRs are now entered on the eCMRs form.
5.6.
The SDR form will be saved into IDM in Syteline under the CMR number.
5.7.
If Enflite requires additional information to address issue, the Supplier will be notified by Manufacturing Engineering requesting further details.
5.8.
If the supplier makes the decision to ship the article without receiving the signed SDR, they are doing so at their own risk and article may be rejected.
5.9.
Manufacturing Engineering shall monitor activities within Syteline regarding CMR’s for newly entered SDRs. Engineering Representative will review issue and complete the disposition. Upon completion, Engineering will notify Quality for further processing.
5.9.1.
The Engineering Representative shall be defined by the MRB Authority Roster, QA-400-044.
5.10.
Quality will complete remaining items on SDR form and the approved form will be sent to Purchasing who will email the completed form and supporting documentation (such as drawings, ECO, NEI, or TS), if applicable, back to the supplier.
5.11.
The Buyer is responsible for adding comments, if required, to the PO showing the details of the SDR.
5.12.
The Supplier must reference the SDR number on the packing list where applicable and attach a copy of the completed and approved SDR with the articles paperwork.
5.13.
A copy of the approved Supplier Deviation Request must accompany the article(s) shipment or the article(s) may be returned to the supplier at the supplier’s expense.
